# E-Commerce Margin Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project investigates the operational and customer behavioural factors contributing to margin compression, declining customer retention, and increasing returns and refunds within an e-commerce business.

The objective is to develop an integrated, data-driven decision-support solution that identifies business performance drivers, predicts potential risks, and recommends actionable interventions.

## Business Objectives

- Investigate the drivers of declining operating margins despite GMV growth.
- Analyse customer retention and repeat purchase behaviour.
- Identify patterns and financial implications of returns and refunds.
- Develop a predictive solution for early risk identification.
- Translate analytical findings and predictions into actionable business recommendations.

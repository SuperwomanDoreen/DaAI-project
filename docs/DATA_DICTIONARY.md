## How to Set Up & Submit Your Team Project

Use this vault strictly as your project brief and dataset download hub. Do not push code to this repository.

1. **Create Your Team Workspace:** Your Team Lead should create a new GitHub repository under their personal account and add all teammates under **Settings -> Collaborators**.
2. **Share the Repository Link:** The Team Lead must share the team repository URL with the DaAI Community via email so we can track progress and review your final submission.
3. **Keep Data Local:** Download the dataset from Google Drive into a local `data/` folder on your computer and add `data/`, `*.db`, `*.csv`, `*.jsonl`, and `*.zip` to your `.gitignore`. Never commit raw data files to GitHub.
4. **Final Submission (3 to 5-Minute Walkthrough Video):** Your team will submit a **3 to 5-minute recorded walkthrough video** (Loom, YouTube unlisted, or Google Drive link) covering the business problem, data pipeline, key insights/model, and recommendations. Link the video directly at the top of your team repository's `README.md`.
5. **Claim Your Personal Copy:** Once the project is complete and the Team Lead sets the repository to **Public**, every teammate can click **Fork** on the top-right of the repository page to copy the finished project onto their own personal GitHub profile.
